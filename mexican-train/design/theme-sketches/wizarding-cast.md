# Wizarding theme: draft cast (for you to edit before it goes into the game)

Same shape as the Lord of the Rings theme: 14 characters, each fixed to a skill level, each with a home, three foods and its own lines.
All lines are original, written in the spirit of each character (no quotes from the books or films). Names are 10 letters or fewer.
Below, each character has a SAMPLE of six of the ~25 line kinds the game uses (great, poor, slow, draw, ownBlocked, invalid). Once you pick the cast, I write the full sets.

| Level | Characters |
|---|---|
| Easy | Neville, Hagrid, Ron, Dobby |
| Normal | Harry, Ginny, Luna, Fred, Draco |
| Hard | McGonagall, Sirius, Snape |
| Expert | Dumbledore, Hermione, Voldemort |

(Spares if you want to swap: Easy Crabbe, Lockhart; Normal George, Cedric, Seamus; Hard Lupin, Moody; Expert Riddle.)

## Easy
**Neville** (home: the greenhouses; food: toad-in-the-hole, treacle tart, mint humbugs)
- great: "Oh! That was brilliant. I would never have thought of that."
- poor: "Hm, I think I would have done that too. Which is not a good sign."
- slow: "Sorry, I do not mean to rush you. I am just holding very still."
- draw: "Off to the boneyard? I will keep your seat. And mine. Mostly mine."
- ownBlocked: "Nothing fits and nothing to draw. I knew I should have stayed in bed."
- invalid: "Oh no, that tile does not go there. I only know because I did it last week."

**Hagrid** (home: the gamekeeper's hut; food: rock cakes, a roast something, a mug of strong tea)
- great: "Now that is a corker of a move! I am proud of yer."
- poor: "Ah, I would not have played that. Then again, I once adopted a dragon."
- slow: "Take yer time. The kettle is on. I might have a biscuit."
- draw: "Drawin' again? Fetch somethin' good, now."
- ownBlocked: "Stuck! Not a tile in sight. Well. That is a shame, innit."
- invalid: "Er, that one does not fit. Look at the numbers, there."

**Ron** (home: the Burrow; food: sausage rolls, a thick sandwich, second helpings)
- great: "Blimey, that was good. Annoying, but good."
- poor: "Ha, even I would not have played that. And I play chess like a man falling downstairs."
- slow: "Are you thinking, or have you fallen asleep on your tiles?"
- draw: "Boneyard, then. Grab me a snack on the way back."
- ownBlocked: "Great. Blocked. Mum would have words about this."
- invalid: "Not there, mate. That never works. I have tried it. Twice."

**Dobby** (home: the castle kitchens; food: roast potatoes, warm bread, a pot of cocoa)
- great: "Dobby thinks that was a very wonderful move. Dobby claps his own hands."
- poor: "Dobby does not wish to say. Dobby is saying it very quietly: oh dear."
- slow: "Dobby can wait. Dobby has waited for much longer, with much worse company."
- draw: "To the boneyard! Dobby hopes there is something kind in there."
- ownBlocked: "Dobby has no tile. Dobby has no draw. Dobby has a very sad ear."
- invalid: "Oh! That does not fit. Dobby will not tell anyone. Dobby promises."

## Notes for the build
- Voices use the same line kinds as the Lord of the Rings theme (great, good, poor, awful, missedOut, slow1-3, draw, pass, doubleResent, ownDraw, ownDrawAgain, ownBlocked, invalid, ownShort, roundBlocked, openBest/Short/None) plus the foods: ~25 kinds x 14 characters.
- Villains and rivals stay good-natured on purpose: a game of dominoes should tease, not threaten.
- Train markers replace the toy train / the ring in the LOTR theme. Backgrounds are applied as `.art-wizard`, like `.art-lotr`.
- Drawn art is generic on purpose (a castle on a crag, floating candles, a red steam engine, a wand): none of the specific emblems, crests or logos, and no drawn characters.

## Decisions so far
- Backgrounds: 2 (hall of floating candles) and 4 (moonlit forest) only. One is picked at random when a game starts (by a separate dice roll, so deals are never touched) and stays for the whole game; hosted games use the host's pick for everyone.
- Train markers: wands only, drawn realistically (see wizarding-wands.png: smooth holly, spiral vine, knotted oak, ebony, elder, cherry; the player colour is the ring or stone). Four players get four different woods.
- Patronus animals (wizarding-patronus.png, rough drafts): stag (Harry), doe (Snape), otter (Hermione), terrier (Ron), hare (Luna), horse (Ginny), phoenix (Dumbledore), cat (McGonagall). A character's animal would flash up behind a speech bubble on a "great" line.
